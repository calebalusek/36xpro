Tips

Keys

2nd turns on the blue label above the next key you press. Press it again to cancel.

Some keys cycle when you press them again: sin → sin⁻¹ → sinh → sinh⁻¹ (cos and tan too), ln log, eˣ 10ˣ, ! nCr nPr, π e i, and x yzt abcd.

▲▼◀▶ move through fraction and exponent boxes, menus and history. On an empty line, ▲ scrolls back through past entries; enter pastes the highlighted one.

◂ ▸ switches the last answer between exact (fractions, √, π) and decimal.

Keyboard

Tab works as 2nd: Tab, then a key, gives its blue function.

Digits, + − * / ^ ( ) ., Enter, Backspace (delete), Esc (clear) and the arrow keys work as you'd expect.

Letter shortcuts: s sin, c cos, t tan, l ln/log, p π, x variables, e EE, f fraction, r √, m mode, ! factorial, % percent.
